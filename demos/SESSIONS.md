# Demo sessions registry

A human-curated registry of named Copilot sessions per demo. Updated
by [`run-demo/start`](../.agents/skills/run-demo/skills/start/SKILL.md)
on first run, and by [`run-demo/resume`](../.agents/skills/run-demo/skills/resume/SKILL.md)
and [`run-demo/recap`](../.agents/skills/run-demo/skills/recap/SKILL.md) on
subsequent runs.

Naming convention: `demo-<id>-<slug-of-title>`, max 32 chars,
kebab-case.
