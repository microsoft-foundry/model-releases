# Mistral

> **Mistral, Mixtral, and Ministral models on Microsoft Foundry.**
> Efficient open-weight and commercial models — including several
> [MoE](../../docs/GLOSSARY.md#m) architectures.

## Why it matters

Mistral models are a go-to when you need strong quality at meaningfully
lower cost than frontier models, or when a mixture-of-experts
architecture gives you frontier-class quality at a smaller *active*
parameter budget.
