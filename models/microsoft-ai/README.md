# Microsoft AI

> **Microsoft-built models** — Phi small language models, MAI models, and
> other first-party Microsoft AI releases available on Microsoft Foundry.

## Why it matters

Microsoft AI models are optimized for cost-efficient, on-device, and
Copilot-adjacent scenarios. Small language models (SLMs) like Phi are
strong candidates when you need speed and price over frontier quality.
