# Contoso Corporate Travel Policy

_Human-readable companion to [`travel-policy.json`](travel-policy.json), which is
the authoritative, machine-enforced version. Version 2027.1 · effective
2026-09-01 · expires 2028-08-31 · amounts in USD._

> Compliance decisions are made from these rules in code — not left to model
> judgement. The concierge may interpret ambiguous requests and explain the
> exception process, but "compliant vs not" comes from the rules below.

## Airfare

- **International business class:** max **$6,000** total. Over-cap requires **VP**
  approval, up to **15%** overspend.
- **International economy:** max **$2,500** total.
- **Domestic business class:** **not allowed**.
- **Domestic economy:** max **$900** total.
- **Cabin rule:** economy is required for flights of **6 hours or less**. Business
  class is allowed only when scheduled flight time **exceeds 6 hours**.
- **Fees rule:** compliance is judged on the **total price including mandatory
  taxes and fees**, not the base fare.

## Hotels

- **Nightly cap by city:** Tokyo $400 · London $380 · Paris $390 · New York $420 ·
  San Francisco $380 · Chicago $300 · everywhere else $300.
- **Preferred chains:** Marriott, Hilton _(a preference, not a hard cap — other
  chains are allowed but should not be chosen over a compliant preferred option)._
- Prefer **city-center** properties.
- **Refundable required.**

## Car rental

- **Maximum class:** midsize (order: economy → compact → midsize → fullsize → suv → luxury).
- **Daily rate cap:** $75.
- **Approved vendors:** Avis, Hertz, Enterprise.

## Booking

- **Advance purchase:** book at least **14 days** ahead.
- **Refundable required.**

## Exceptions

- **Refundable waiver:** allowed for **Executive** level (no approver needed).
- **International business over cap:** **VP** approval, up to 15% overspend.
- **Advance-purchase waiver:** **Manager** approval for urgent business travel
  inside the 14-day window.
