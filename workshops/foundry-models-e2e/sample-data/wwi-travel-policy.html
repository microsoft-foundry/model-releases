Travel & Expense Policy

Booking windows, per-trip caps, reimbursable categories, hotel and flight rules, and the receipt-submission process for all WWI full-time employees travelling on company business.

Owner: People Ops · Travel-Ops Audited monthly (10% sample) Approvals: Manager · VP · CFO
At a glance Ten sections cover scope, booking windows, per-trip caps, reimbursable categories (ground transport · parking · meals · Wi-Fi), non-reimbursable items, hotels, flights, client entertainment, receipt submission, and exceptions.

§ 2Booking Window

Domestic trips must be booked at least 7 days in advance. International trips must be booked at least 21 days in advance. Exceptions require manager + travel-ops approval.

§ 3Per-Trip Budget Caps

Caps include flight + hotel + ground transport. Over by ≤ 15 % requires VP approval; over by > 15 % requires CFO approval.

§ 4 · Reimbursable

  • 4.2Airport parking up to $25/day; receipt within 30 days.
  • 4.3Per-diem meals: $75 dom · $95 int’l. Alcohol only for client entertainment.
  • 4.4In-flight Wi-Fi on flights ≥ 2 h; hotel Wi-Fi when not complimentary.

§ 7 · Flights — Class of Service

  • 7.1Economy is the default.
  • 7.1Premium economy allowed on flights ≥ 6 h.
  • 7.1Business only for flights > 6 h and with VP approval at booking.
  • 7.1First class is never reimbursable.
